## Price provenance

- YouTube's official [plan index](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16475192) identifies Individual, Family, Student, Premium Lite, and pre-paid annual plans; its official [annual-plan help page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11417260?hl=en) lists the United States and confirms annual plans are for individual users.
- The public `youtube.com/premium` page is region-rendered; it did not expose US amounts from this research network. The current US rates above were cross-checked against the April 2026 price-change reports: [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/10/youtube-premium-price-increase/) for Individual, Family, and Annual, plus [PiunikaWeb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/04/10/youtube-premium-price-increase-us-2026/) for Student and Premium Lite. No promotion, trial, or eligibility price is included.
