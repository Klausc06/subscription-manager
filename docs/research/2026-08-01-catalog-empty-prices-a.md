## Evidence notes

- **Second-pass correction:** a missing anonymous price on a landing page is
  not a removal criterion. The Chinese rows added in the second pass use the
  provider's public Apple App Store IAP/auto-renewal text; NYT uses its public
  US App Store IAP menu; Apple Music uses its public CN pricing page.
- **Remaining REMOVE:** `dushu` remains because the name/domain did not identify
  one unambiguous fixed-price recurring product; `douyin` remains because the
  anonymous public App Store/web material exposed virtual-currency purchases,
  not a named fixed recurring membership plan.
- Markets and currencies are deliberately not normalized: the iCloud+ and PS
  Plus evidence is US/USD, while China App Store evidence is CN/CNY.
